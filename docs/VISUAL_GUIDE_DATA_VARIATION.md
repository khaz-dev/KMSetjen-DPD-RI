When you visited `/articles`, all article cards looked like this:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Article Title 1                         │
│ Article excerpt...                      │
│ Author | 2 hari yang lalu | 👁 0 | ❤️ 0 │
└─────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Article Title 2                         │
│ Article excerpt...                      │
│ Author | 2 hari yang lalu | 👁 0 | ❤️ 0 │
└─────────────────────────────────────────┘

┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
```

**Problem:** Every article was identical. When you selected different sort options, the **order** changed but you couldn't **see** the change because all metrics were the same!

   - 👁 Notice: Likes count is now 2nd (bold + blue)
   - 👁 Notice: Views and date moved around again

   - Article metrics translations update
   - Reordering and highlighting still works
